# BlindSpot
### Discover the work behind the work.

## Overview
BlindSpot is a domain-agnostic AI engine designed to uncover hidden operational inefficiencies that humans often overlook. It investigates repetitive tasks, unnecessary handoffs, dependencies, and the underlying reasons behind wasted time.

Unlike conventional tools that track known problems, BlindSpot aims to discover invisible patterns in operational data and turn them into actionable insights.

## The Problem
Businesses lose valuable time to repetitive work and inefficient processes that accumulate unnoticed. Organizations may automate visible tasks without understanding why the work exists or whether automation is the right solution.

## Our Solution
BlindSpot analyzes operational data to investigate hidden work and determine whether activities should be:
- Automated
- AI-assisted
- Redesigned
- Integrated
- Kept under human control

Because not everything that can be automated should be. Human judgment must remain where it matters most.

## Intervention Simulation
BlindSpot includes a scenario simulator that allows users to adjust assumptions and explore potential changes in handling time, affected cases, and recovered capacity before implementing an intervention.

**Note:** Scenario outputs are estimates based on user-controlled assumptions, not validated real-world savings.

## Core Vision
Turn invisible inefficiency into actionable intelligence—helping organizations reclaim wasted time without sacrificing human judgment.
